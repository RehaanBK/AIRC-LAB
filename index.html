Governance & Policy

Professor Alignment Meeting Agenda

While this control center standardizes tracking, UID card verification, and 3D print logging, schedule a 15-minute alignment meeting with the professor to confirm:

  • Maximum Borrow Duration & Extensions: Standard loan window (e.g. 7 days for sensors/boards vs. semester-long for Senior Design teams).
  • Restricted High-Value Equipment: Confirm which robots (Hexapod, WidowX/Troseen arms, ROSbots) require written professor approval before checkout.
  • Overdue & Damaged Hardware Policy: Protocol when items are returned late or damaged (e.g. LiPo batteries, RealSense cameras).
  • Consumables vs. Returnable Tools: Separate tracking for expendables (zip ties, tape, resistors, filament) vs. serialized hardware.
  • 3D Print Quotas & Priority: Filament weight limits per student/team and priority rules for capstone/competition deadlines.
  • University IT / RFID Reader Format: Confirm whether the campus ID card reader outputs decimal student ID numbers or hex badge UIDs.
